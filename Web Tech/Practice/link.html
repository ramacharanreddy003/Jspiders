Sai Pallavi

Sai Pallavi Senthamarai Kannan (pronounced [saɪ paˈləvi]; born 9 May 1992) is an Indian actress who primarily works in Telugu, Tamil and Malayalam films. Pallavi has received several accolades, including a Tamil Nadu State Film Award, seven Filmfare Awards South and three SIIMA Awards. She was honoured with Kalaimamani in the year 2021 by the Government of Tamil Nadu.[3] Pallavi's acting journey began with uncredited roles and dance show appearances, but her breakthrough came as Malar in the 2015 Malayalam film Premam. While a medical student, her acclaimed performance earned her a Filmfare Award for Best Female Debut – South, launching her into the South Indian film industry. In 2017, she ventured into Telugu cinema with Fidaa, winning the Filmfare Award for Best Actress – Telugu.[4] Pallavi has starred in the films Kali (2016), Middle Class Abbayi (2017), Maari 2 (2018), Paava Kadhaigal (2020), Love Story (2021), Shyam Singha Roy (2021), Gargi (2022), Amaran (2024) and Thandel (2025). Her song "Rowdy Baby" is the first South Indian video song to reach one billion views on YouTube.[5] Her performances in Gargi, Amaran and Love Story earned the Filmfare Award for Best Actress in Tamil and Telugu, respectively, while her work in Shyam Singha Roy and Virata Parvam (2022) gained her the Filmfare Critics Award for Best Actress – Telugu.[6] She was featured by Forbes magazine as one of India's 30 Under 30 in 2020.[7] Early life and work Sai Pallavi was born on 9 May 1992[8] in a Badaga family[9] family in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. Her hometown is Kotagiri in the Nilgiris district, Tamil Nadu. She was born to Senthamarai Kannan, a central excise officer (often associated with police and customs work) also a football player, and Radha Kannan, a dancer. Pallavi herself is an avid badminton player. She has a younger sister, Pooja Kannan, who has also followed in her footsteps, working as an actress in small roles and making her debut in the Tamil film Chithirai Sevvaanam which was released in the year 2023. Pallavi was raised and educated in Coimbatore, doing her schooling at Avila Convent School.[10][11][12] She pursued a career in medicine, graduating in 2016 from the Tbilisi State Medical University in Georgia, which is recognised by the Medical Council of India.[13][14] She successfully took her Foreign Medical Graduate Examination (FMGE) on 31 August 2020 in Trichy.[15] [16] However, despite officially being a doctor, she has not yet registered as a medical practitioner in India, choosing instead to focus entirely on her flourishing career as an actress.[17] Besides her native Badaga, Pallavi is multilingual and is fluent in Tamil, English, Telugu, Hindi, Malayalam, Kannada and Georgian.[18] Acting career Early career (2005–2014) Pallavi's initial foray into the movie industry began at the bottom. Before getting her big break, a young Pallavi worked in uncredited roles as a background dancer and a junior side-artist. She made her first on-screen appearance as a child artist in the Tamil film Kasthuri Maan (2005). Later, she bunked school to work as a junior artist in the Jayam Ravi and Kangana Ranaut-starrer Dhaam Dhoom (2008), where she can be spotted in the background. Her early dance career started with grueling reality TV competitions. In 2008, she participated in the Tamil dance reality show Ungalil Yaar Adutha Prabhu Deva on Vijay TV. The following year, in 2009, she entered the Telugu television space and participated in the Dhee Ultimate Dance Show (D4) on ETV where she reached the finals of the show.[19][20][21] [22] During this early struggle, she also appeared in a few television advertisements, one of her most notable early commercials was for Profit, a nutritional drink. and starred in a short film titled Kaatchi Pizhai.[23][24][25]
